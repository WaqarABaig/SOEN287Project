HOW A REQUEST WORKS

  1. 01

    SEND YOUR REQUEST

    Pick a lab, name the test and standard, describe your sample and attach your spec sheet as a PDF.

  2. 02

    GET A QUOTE

    The lab replies with a fixed price and turnaround. Nothing is charged until you accept.

  3. 03

    ACCEPT AND SEND YOUR SAMPLE

    Your payment is held by OpenLab. Drop off or mail your sample using the lab's instructions.

  4. 04

    RECEIVE YOUR RESULTS

    The lab posts a summary and a report PDF to your dashboard. Only then is the lab paid.
